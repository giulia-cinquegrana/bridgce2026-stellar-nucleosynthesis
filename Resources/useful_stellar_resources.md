# Useful resources for stellar physics and nucleosynthesis

## AGB reviews

- Busso, M., Gallino, R. and Wasserburg, G.J., 1999. *Nucleosynthesis in Asymptotic Giant Branch Stars: Relevance for Galactic Enrichment and Solar System Formation*. Annual Review of Astronomy and Astrophysics, 37(1), pp.239-309.
- Herwig, F., 2005. *Evolution of Asymptotic Giant Branch Stars*. Annu. Rev. Astron. Astrophys., 43(1), pp.435-479. 
- Karakas, A.I. and Lattanzio, J.C., 2014. *The Dawes Review 2: Nucleosynthesis and Stellar Yields of Low-and Intermediate-Mass Single Stars*. Publications of the Astronomical Society of Australia, 31, p.e030.

## Nucleosynthesis reviews

- Lugaro, M., Pignatari, M., Reifarth, R. and Wiescher, M., 2023. *The s-Process and Beyond*. Annual Review of Nuclear and Particle Science, 73(1), pp.315-340.
- Nomoto, K.I., Kobayashi, C. and Tominaga, N., 2013. *Nucleosynthesis in Stars and the Chemical Enrichment of Galaxies*. Annual Review of Astronomy and Astrophysics, 51, pp.457-509.
- Arcones, A. and Thielemann, F.K., 2023. *Origin of the Elements*. A. Arcones, F. Thielemann. The Astronomy and astrophysics review, 31(1), p.1.

## Stellar evolution and nucleosynthesis textbooks and lecture notes

- Onno Pols' lecture notes: https://www.astro.ru.nl/~onnop/education/stev_utrecht_notes/
- Kippenhahn, R., Weigert, A. and Weiss, A., 1990. *Stellar structure and evolution* (Vol. 192). Berlin: Springer-verlag.

## Nuclear astrophysics textbooks

- Krane, K.S., 1991. *Introductory nuclear physics*. John Wiley & Sons.
